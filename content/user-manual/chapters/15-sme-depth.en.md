# SME depth (Phases 13–16)

## Fixed assets

Open **Accounting → Fixed assets**. Add a category (cost, accumulated depreciation, expense accounts), then an asset card. Use **Depreciate** for a calendar month to post straight-line journals.

## Equity

**Accounting → Equity** holds the shareholders register and contribution / draw / dividend events. **Reports → Consolidation** sums P&L across parent and child companies (journals whose memo starts with `[IC]` are skipped).

## Recruitment

**Employees → Recruitment** is a pipeline: New → Screening → Interview → Offer → Hire. Appraisals take a score and goals. Left employees cannot join a new payroll run.

**Customers → Pipeline** holds opportunities (Lead → Qualified → Proposal → Won/Lost) and follow-up activities.

## Collaboration apps

Turn on Sign, Appointments, Discuss, Planning, or Surveys under **Apps & features**. Sign sends a public link (`/sign-request/…`). Appointments refuse overlapping bookings. Discuss supports threads and pins. Planning stores hours per person per day. Surveys can close and show a tally.
