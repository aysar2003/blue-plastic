Isticmaal kuwan marka shirkaddu app-ka furto.

### Approvals

[Approvals](/approvals) — documents sugaya print/post approval. Ka shaqee **Pending**, ka dibna taariikhda approved. Workflows (haddii la dejiyey) waxay qeexaan cidda saxeexa.

### Projects

[Projects](/projects) — folder shaqo oo ku xidha estimates, sales orders, invoices, POs, iyo bills hal shaqo macmiil.

**Waqtiga (time):** Ku diiwaangeli saacadaha mashruuca (billable rate + cost rate doorasho). Fur [WIP & retainers](/projects/wip) si aad ugu soo saarto invoice waqtiga aan la bixin, ama invoice cusub → **Add billable time**.

**Dhismaha:** [Progress](/projects/progress) waxay xisaabisaa % qandaraaska; [Contracts](/projects/contracts) milestones. Budget / contract / retainer ka beddel foomka mashruuca.

**Tasks:** Kanban oo leh subtasks, blockers, iyo Gantt (fur mashruuc → **Gantt**). [My tasks](/projects/my-tasks) iyo [My timesheets](/projects/timesheets). [Planning](/planning) waxay u qorsheysaa dadka maalmaha.

**Lacag & class:** Job P&L waxay u beddeshaa lacagaha ajnabiga base-ka shirkadda. Marka mashruuc lagu xidho iib ama bill, class-ka analytical wuu buuxsamaa haddii uu madhan yahay. POS waxay ku calaamadin kartaa iib mashruuc. Macmiil portal → Projects.

Reports: [Projects → Reports](/projects/reports).
