# Heerka SME (Phases 13–16)

## Hantida go'an (Fixed assets)

Fur **Accounting → Fixed assets**. Ku dar category (cost, accum. dep., expense), ka dib asset. **Depreciate** bil ahaan ayaa u posta journal-ka straight-line.

## Equity

**Accounting → Equity** waa diiwaanka shareholders iyo contribution / draw / dividend. **Reports → Consolidation** waxay isu geysaa P&L parent + children (journal memo `[IC]` waa la dhaafaa).

## Shaqaalaysi

**Employees → Recruitment** waa pipeline: New → Screening → Interview → Offer → Hire. Appraisals waxay qaataan score iyo goals. Shaqaalaha LEFT lama geliyo payroll cusub.

**Customers → Pipeline** waa fursadaha iibka (Lead → Qualified → Proposal → Won/Lost) iyo follow-up.

## Apps wadashaqayn

Fur Sign, Appointments, Discuss, Planning, ama Surveys ka **Apps & features**. Sign waxay dirtaa link dadweyne. Appointments ma oggola waqti is-dul-saaran. Discuss waa threads + pins. Planning waxay kaydisaa saacadaha qofka. Surveys waa la xidhi karaa oo natiijada la arki karaa.
