Use these when the company has turned the app on.

### Approvals

[Approvals](/approvals) — documents waiting for print/post approval. Work **Pending**, then browse approved history. Workflows (if configured) define who must sign off.

### Projects

[Projects](/projects) — job folders linking estimates, sales orders, invoices, purchase orders, and bills for one customer job.

**Bill time:** Log hours on the project (billable rate + optional cost rate). Open [WIP & retainers](/projects/wip) to invoice unbilled time, or on a new invoice use **Add billable time**.

**Construction:** [Progress](/projects/progress) bills % of contract; [Contracts](/projects/contracts) bills milestones. Set budget / contract / retainer on the project edit form.

**Tasks:** Kanban board with subtasks, blockers, and [Gantt](/projects) timeline (open a project → **Gantt**). [My tasks](/projects/my-tasks) and [My timesheets](/projects/timesheets) list your work. [Planning](/planning) books people by day.

**Money & class:** Job P&L converts foreign-currency sales/purchases to company base. Linking a project on a sale or bill fills the analytical class when blank. POS can tag a sale to a project. Customers with portal access see [Projects](/portal/projects).

Reports: [Projects → Reports](/projects/reports) and Job profitability.

### Documents & knowledge

[Documents](/documents) — company file library. [Knowledge](/knowledge) — internal wiki / how-tos for your organisation’s own procedures (separate from this Help manual).

### To-do and calendar

[To-do](/todo) and [Calendar](/calendar) — personal/team tasks and reminders; they do not post to the ledger.
